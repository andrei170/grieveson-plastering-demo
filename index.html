On your current site it appears once, in the middle of a paragraph. Here is what it could look like when it is doing some work.

British Gypsum Certified

A manufacturer put its name to our work.

Anyone can call themselves a plasterer. A manufacturer certification is different, because it is somebody else vouching for the work rather than us describing ourselves as reliable.

For a homeowner it means the finish should hold up. For a builder or a commercial client choosing between three quotes, it is often the thing that makes the decision easy — because it is checkable and the other two quotes usually have nothing like it.

Third-party, not self-declaredIt is a manufacturer’s scheme, not a claim we wrote about ourselves.

Often a repair will do, and it is the smaller job. Saying so in writing is worth more than it costs you, because it is the thing that makes somebody trust the quote they get.
